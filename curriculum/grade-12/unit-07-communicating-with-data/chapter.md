# Grade 12 · Unit 7: Communicating with data

> Track: A/B · Tools: charts, writing, optional Streamlit · Lessons: 3
> Datasets: `census-districts`

**Big idea.** Analysis only matters if people understand and can act on it: action titles, honest charts, short memos and clear limits.

**What you will be able to do**

* I can write an action title and caption.
* I can write a one-page policy memo.
* I can present uncertainty and limits.

**Words to know:** data story, action title, policy memo, audience, limit (Nepali glossary: `curriculum/framework/glossary.md`).

**Notebook:** `notebooks/data_story.ipynb` (open in JupyterLite, Colab or Jupyter; runs offline on the synthetic table).

## Lesson 1: Action titles and honest charts

**Hook (5 min).** Which headline makes you want to read: 'Chart 3' or 'Girls' enrolment is falling in mountain districts'?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** State the message in the title, label units and sources, use the right chart, keep the baseline honest, mention the limit.

**Activity (PRIMM: Predict → Run → Investigate → Modify → Make) (20 min).** Rewrite 5 chart titles as action titles; redraw one chart.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What is an action title?
2. What goes in the caption?
3. Why a zero baseline for bars?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 2: The one-page memo

**Hook (5 min).** A ward chair has 3 minutes. What do you give them?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Bottom line first, evidence, uncertainty, recommended action. Plain words, short sentences.

**Activity (20 min).** Draft a 250-word memo; swap and have a classmate summarise it back.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What goes first?
2. Why say what you don't know?
3. Who is your audience?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 3: Presenting and publishing

**Hook (5 min).** How do you present uncertainty without confusing people?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Use ranges and plain words ('likely between…'); show one main chart; invite questions; publish with licence and credits. Optional: dashboard (Streamlit) or Django web page.

**Activity (20 min).** Give a 3-minute talk with one chart and one limit; collect feedback.

**Check (5 min).**
1. How to show uncertainty plainly?
2. What do you publish with data work?
3. Is Django required?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?
