# Unit project — Choose the threshold

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
A clinic can follow up at most N mothers.

## Plan
Define costs of each error.

## Data
Synthetic health table.

## Analysis
Metrics at 5 thresholds.

## Conclusion
Recommend a threshold with caveats.

## Hand in
Notebook + recommendation memo.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
