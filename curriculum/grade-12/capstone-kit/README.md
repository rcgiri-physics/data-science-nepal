# Grade 12 capstone kit

A capstone serves a **client** (ward office, health post, school, NGO, cooperative) with a real question.
Use these files in order:

1. [`client-brief.md`](client-brief.md) — scope the question and the deliverables.
2. [`data-agreement-template.md`](data-agreement-template.md) — what data may be used, stored, shared and deleted.
3. Analysis notebook: `../unit-08-capstone-with-a-client/notebooks/capstone_template.ipynb`.
4. [`model-card-template.md`](model-card-template.md) — purpose, data, performance, limits, groups affected.
5. [`rubric.md`](rubric.md) — marking guide.

**Safety rules:** students never receive personally identifying data about real people without the client's written
agreement, a teacher's approval and guardian consent where students appear. Prefer aggregated or synthetic data.
A signed agreement is not legal advice; ask the school or a lawyer about local requirements.
