# Grade 12 · Unit 8: Capstone with a community client

> Track: B/A (project) · Tools: any (Python, spreadsheet, Streamlit); Git · Lessons: 3 · Version: 0.1 draft (English)
> Datasets: `health-synthetic`, `census-districts`, `kathmandu-air-quality`, `climate-monthly`

**Big idea.** Real data science serves someone: a client with a question, a data agreement, responsible handling, and a public write-up.

**What you will be able to do**

* I can scope a question with a client.
* I can agree what data may be used and shared.
* I can deliver an analysis, model card and write-up.

**Words to know:** client, scope, data agreement, model card, deliverable (Nepali glossary: `curriculum/framework/glossary.md`).

**Notebook:** `notebooks/capstone_template.ipynb` (open in JupyterLite, Colab or Jupyter; runs offline on the synthetic table).

## Lesson 1: Scope and agree

**Hook (5 min).** A health post asks: 'Who should we visit first?' What do you ask back?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Clarify the decision, the data you may use, what success means, and what you will deliver. Write a data agreement: purpose, consent, storage, sharing, deletion.

**Activity (20 min).** Role-play a client meeting; complete the client brief and data agreement from the kit.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What does a data agreement cover?
2. Why define success first?
3. May you share a client's data publicly?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 2: Build and audit

**Hook (5 min).** Is your model better than the client's current way?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Compare with the current practice and a baseline, audit across groups, and write a model card (purpose, data, metrics, limits, who is affected).

**Activity (PRIMM: Predict → Run → Investigate → Modify → Make) (20 min).** Complete the analysis, group audit and model card.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What goes in a model card?
2. What is the client's 'current way'?
3. Why audit before handover?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 3: Hand over and publish

**Hook (5 min).** Will the client understand and use your work?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Present to the client in plain language, hand over the notebook/dashboard with a README, and publish an anonymised write-up with consent.

**Activity (PRIMM: Predict → Run → Investigate → Modify → Make) (20 min).** Present, collect feedback, and publish the write-up in `community/student-gallery/` (with consent).

**Check (5 min).**
1. What must be anonymised before publishing?
2. What goes in the README?
3. Whose consent is required?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?
