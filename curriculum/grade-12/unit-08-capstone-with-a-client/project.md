# Unit project — Community capstone

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
A real or realistic question from a community client.

## Plan
Client brief + data agreement.

## Data
Agreed dataset(s) with licences.

## Analysis
Baseline, model/rule, audit.

## Conclusion
Write-up, model card, handover.

## Hand in
Repo/folder, write-up, model card, 5-minute presentation (Grade 12 flagship).

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
