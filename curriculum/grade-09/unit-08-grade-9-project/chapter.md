# Grade 9 · Unit 8: Grade 9 project: which districts are changing?

> Track: A/B (project) · Tools: spreadsheet or SQL/pandas · Lessons: 2 · Version: 0.1 draft (English)
> Datasets: `census-districts`, `kathmandu-air-quality`

**Big idea.** Put the PPDAC cycle together: ask, plan, wrangle, analyse and communicate a story from a district table.

**What you will be able to do**

* I can run a whole investigation from question to poster.
* I can document data cleaning and choices.
* I can present findings with a limit.

**Words to know:** PPDAC, claim, evidence, limit, reproducible (Nepali glossary: `curriculum/framework/glossary.md`).

**Notebook:** `notebooks/project_template.ipynb` (open in JupyterLite, Colab or Jupyter; runs offline on the synthetic table).

---

## Lesson 1: Plan and clean

**Hook (5 min).** A good project fits in one sentence. What is yours?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Write the question, choose variables and a rate, list risks, then clean the data and keep a log.

**Activity (20 min).** Submit a plan card (question, variables, rate, risks); clean your table and write the log.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What does a plan card contain?
2. Why log cleaning steps?
3. Name one risk in district tables.

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 2: Analyse and present

**Hook (5 min).** Two minutes to convince your class: what is your claim?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Analyse with the planned queries; choose the strongest chart; state the claim, the evidence and one limit; practise the talk.

**Activity (20 min).** Build the poster and give a 2-minute talk with peer feedback (two stars and a wish); do the oral check.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What are the three parts of a conclusion?
2. Why an oral check?
3. What goes in the limit box?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?
