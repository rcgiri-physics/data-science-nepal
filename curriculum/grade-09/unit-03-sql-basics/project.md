# Unit project — Which districts are changing?

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
Pick an indicator (e.g. people abroad per 1,000, girls' share of enrolment) and find where it is highest and lowest.

## Plan
Write the question; list the columns and tables; decide on a rate (not a count).

## Data
Census-style district table + schools table (synthetic, or real after fetching).

## Analysis
Three SQL queries including one GROUP BY and one JOIN; one chart or table.

## Conclusion
Poster with claim, evidence, reasoning, one limit and data source.

## Hand in
Notebook + one-page poster (the Grade 9 flagship).

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
