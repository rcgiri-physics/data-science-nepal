# Unit project — Spreadsheet fact sheet

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
What are three facts about the districts in the table?

## Plan
Choose three questions that sorting, formulas or pivots can answer.

## Data
District table (synthetic or real).

## Analysis
Use formulas and a pivot table.

## Conclusion
Present three facts with numbers and the formula behind each.

## Hand in
Spreadsheet file + 3-sentence summary.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
