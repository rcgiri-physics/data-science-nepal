# Grade 8 — Data detectives (no code)

**Tools:** paper, calculator, spreadsheet, CODAP · **Units:** 8 · **Lessons:** 24 (+ projects)

Grade 8 builds the habit of a **statistical investigation** without any programming. Students ask a question,
collect or read a small table (their own class, plus one district-level table), summarise it, and defend a claim with
evidence. It adds what the compulsory Maths statistics chapter usually lacks: forming questions, fair collection,
interpretation, and data ethics.
