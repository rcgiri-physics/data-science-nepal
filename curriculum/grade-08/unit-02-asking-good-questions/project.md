# Unit project — Our survey plan

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
Choose one question about your class or school that is statistical.

## Plan
Write the exact survey question, answer options, who you will ask and how you will pick them fairly.

## Data
Pilot with 5 classmates; fix confusing wording.

## Analysis
Count responses; check if any option is missing.

## Conclusion
Explain one bias risk and how you reduced it.

## Hand in
Survey sheet + one-paragraph plan.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
