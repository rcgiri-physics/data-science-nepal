# Teacher guide — Grade 8, Unit 8: Data and people

**Time:** 3 lessons + project (about 5 periods). **Track:** A (no-code).

## Before you start
* Materials: Short case cards (printed), chart paper.
* Read `docs/pedagogy/lesson-anatomy.md` and `docs/pedagogy/ai-use-policy.md`.

## If you have no computers / no internet
All eight units run on paper, calculators and the blackboard. Computers are optional (spreadsheet or CODAP, https://codap.concord.org); every activity below has a paper version.

## Common misconceptions
* "If it is anonymous it can't hurt anyone." Small groups can still be identified.
* "Data are neutral." Who collects, what is asked and who answers all shape data.

## Differentiation
* Support: give a partially filled table / sentence starters; pair with a data-steward role.
* Stretch: ask "what would change your conclusion?" and "who is missing?"

## Answer key (check questions)
**Lesson 1: Who is missing?**
1. Who might an online poll miss?  
   *Answer:* People without internet or devices.
2. Why does missing data matter?  
   *Answer:* It can make the results unrepresentative and decisions unfair.
3. How could you include more people?  
   *Answer:* Use several methods: paper, in person, visits.

**Lesson 2: Privacy and sensitive data**
1. Give two sensitive data fields.  
   *Answer:* e.g. family income, religion, disability, health, exact home location.
2. What does 'collect only what you need' mean?  
   *Answer:* Do not ask for things your question doesn't require.
3. Small group, 'anonymous' data — risk?  
   *Answer:* People may still be recognised, e.g. 'the only girl who cycles from ward 4'.

**Lesson 3: Fair or unfair?**
1. Name one question to ask about any data-based decision.  
   *Answer:* Who is missing / who benefits / who could be harmed / how was it collected.
2. Is data always neutral?  
   *Answer:* No: choices in collecting and measuring shape it.
3. Write one rule for our class data charter.  
   *Answer:* e.g. never share names; always ask consent.

## Real-data exercises: notes
* **R1** Census 2021 reported an absent population of about 2.2 million (check the results page for the exact
  value and definition). Counting them matters for remittances, labour and planning; still missed: people who
  left the whole household, irregular migrants, people the respondent forgets.
* **R2** In many municipalities OSM is good for roads and patchy for buildings and services. Discuss "unmapped is
  not absent".
* **R3** Most stations are in the Hill and Terai, few at high altitude. Statements about "Nepal's rainfall" really
  describe places where stations exist.
