# Unit project — Our data charter

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
How should our class collect and share data responsibly?

## Plan
Review the survey you ran in Unit 3.

## Data
Use your class table and consent sheet.

## Analysis
Audit: what sensitive data did we collect? who might be missing? were codes used?

## Conclusion
Write a 6-point class data charter and sign it.

## Hand in
Charter poster + 3 improvements you'd make to your survey.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
