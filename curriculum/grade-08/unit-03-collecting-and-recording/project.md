# Unit project — Collect our class data

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
Run your planned survey (from Unit 2) with the class.

## Plan
Print the sheet; agree codes and units; decide who records.

## Data
Collect with consent; record in a tidy table of at least 5 variables.

## Analysis
Make frequency tables for each categorical variable.

## Conclusion
List two data problems you noticed (blank, mixed units) and how you fixed them.

## Hand in
Tidy class table + frequency tables.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
