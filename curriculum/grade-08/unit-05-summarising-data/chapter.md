# Grade 8 · Unit 5: Summarising data

> Track: A (no-code) · Tools: paper, calculator · Lessons: 3
> Datasets: `class-survey-synthetic`

**Big idea.** One number can summarise a whole list — but which number depends on the shape of the data and the question.

**What you will be able to do**

* I can find mean, median, mode and range.
* I can choose the best summary for a skewed list.
* I can explain what a summary hides.

**Words to know:** mean, median, mode, range, outlier, spread (Nepali glossary: `curriculum/framework/glossary.md`).

---

## Lesson 1: Mean, median and mode

**Hook (5 min).** Five students' pocket money: 20, 20, 30, 40, 90 rupees. What is a 'typical' amount?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Mean = sum ÷ count. Median = middle value when sorted. Mode = most frequent. Range = largest − smallest.

**Activity (20 min).** Compute all four for the pocket-money list; then for the class's travel_minutes. Line up as a 'human median' by sorting yourselves by minutes.

**Check (5 min).**
1. Mean of 20, 20, 30, 40, 90?
2. Median of 20, 20, 30, 40, 90?
3. Mode and range?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 2: Which summary when?

**Hook (5 min).** A village has 9 families earning about 15,000 rupees a month and one earning 300,000. What is the 'average' income?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Outliers pull the mean. When data are skewed or have outliers the median describes a typical value better. For categories use the mode.

**Activity (20 min).** Compute mean and median with and without the outlier family; discuss which one a newspaper should report and why.

**Check (5 min).**
1. Incomes: 9 × 15,000 and 1 × 300,000 — mean?
2. Median of the same list?
3. Which is more typical and why?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 3: Spread and what summaries hide

**Hook (5 min).** Two classes both have mean 8 hours of sleep. Are they the same?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Two groups can have the same mean but different spread. Always look at the graph as well as the summary.

**Activity (20 min).** Class A sleeps 7, 8, 8, 9; class B sleeps 5, 6, 10, 11. Find means and ranges, draw both dot plots, and describe the difference in a sentence.

**Check (5 min).**
1. Mean of class A (7, 8, 8, 9)?
2. Mean and range of class B (5, 6, 10, 11)?
3. What do these tell us that the means alone do not?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?
