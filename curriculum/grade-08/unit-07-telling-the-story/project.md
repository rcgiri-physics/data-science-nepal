# Unit project — Our class, our school poster

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
What is one true, interesting thing about our class or school?

## Plan
Pick one claim you can support with at least two pieces of evidence (one from the class table, one from the district table).

## Data
Class table + district table extract.

## Analysis
Create graphs and summaries.

## Conclusion
Poster with claim, evidence, reasoning, limits, sources.

## Hand in
A one-page poster and a 1-minute talk (the Grade 8 flagship project).

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
