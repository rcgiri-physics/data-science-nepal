# Teacher guide — Grade 8, Unit 7: Telling the story

**Time:** 3 lessons + project (about 5 periods). **Track:** A (no-code).

| # | Lesson | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claim, evidence, reasoning | 40–45 min |
| 2 | Poster design | 40–45 min |
| 3 | Feedback and presenting | 40–45 min |
| P | Unit project: Our class, our school poster | 2 periods |

## Before you start
* Materials: Poster paper, markers, sticky notes for feedback.
* Read `docs/pedagogy/lesson-anatomy.md` and `docs/pedagogy/ai-use-policy.md`.

## If you have no computers / no internet
All eight units run on paper, calculators and the blackboard. Computers are optional (spreadsheet or CODAP, https://codap.concord.org); every activity below has a paper version.

## Common misconceptions
* A poster full of graphs without a claim. Every graph needs a reason to be there.
* Claims that go beyond the data ("all students" from 10 classmates).

## Differentiation
* Support: give a partially filled table / sentence starters; pair with a data-steward role.
* Stretch: ask "what would change your conclusion?" and "who is missing?"

## Answer key (check questions)
**Lesson 1: Claim, evidence, reasoning**
1. What is evidence?  
   *Answer:* Data (numbers, graph) that support the claim.
2. What does reasoning add?  
   *Answer:* It explains how the evidence supports the claim and its limits.
3. 10 classmates walk. Claim: 'Nepali students walk to school.' OK?  
   *Answer:* No: sample is too small and not fair; claim too general.

**Lesson 2: Poster design**
1. What goes in the headline?  
   *Answer:* The main message/claim.
2. Name two things every graph needs.  
   *Answer:* Title and labelled axes (with units).
3. Why add a 'what we cannot say' box?  
   *Answer:* To be honest about limits and avoid overclaiming.

**Lesson 3: Feedback and presenting**
1. What is a 'wish' in feedback?  
   *Answer:* A specific suggestion for improvement.
2. What three things go in a 1-minute talk?  
   *Answer:* Claim, evidence, one limit.
3. Why avoid saying only 'good' or 'bad'?  
   *Answer:* It does not help the person improve.

## Assessment
Starter quiz each lesson (3 questions, one recalling the previous lesson); unit quiz in `quiz.yml`;
project marked with the rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`; 2-minute oral check per group.
