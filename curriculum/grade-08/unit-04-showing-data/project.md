# Unit project — Graph gallery

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
What does our class data look like?

## Plan
Choose three graphs (one bar, one dot plot or line, one of your choice) for three variables.

## Data
Use the tidy class table.

## Analysis
Draw them carefully (paper or CODAP), with titles and units.

## Conclusion
Write one sentence per graph: what it shows and one thing it hides.

## Hand in
Three graphs + captions displayed as a gallery walk.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
