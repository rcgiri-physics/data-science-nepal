# Unit project — Two variables, one honest line

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
Pick two variables that may be related.

## Plan
Predict the relationship.

## Data
Air or climate table.

## Analysis
Scatter, r, line, residuals.

## Conclusion
Interpret slope; list a confounder; avoid causal language.

## Hand in
Notebook + paragraph.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
