# Grade 10 · Unit 8: Project: Air in my city

> Track: B (project) · Tools: Python, pandas, matplotlib, optional Streamlit · Lessons: 2 · Version: 0.1 draft (English)
> Datasets: `kathmandu-air-quality`

**Big idea.** Run the whole PPDAC cycle on a year of PM2.5: clean, analyse, conclude honestly and publish.

**What you will be able to do**

* I can run an end-to-end analysis.
* I can communicate with a chart and a dashboard.
* I can state limits and uncertainties.

**Words to know:** PPDAC, WHO guideline, exceedance, limit (Nepali glossary: `curriculum/framework/glossary.md`).

**Notebook:** `notebooks/air_in_my_city.ipynb` (open in JupyterLite, Colab or Jupyter; runs offline on the synthetic table).

---

## Lesson 1: Plan and clean

**Hook (5 min).** A good question names the place, the pollutant and the period. What's yours?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Write the question, choose a limit value (and cite it), plan for missing days, clean and log.

**Activity (PRIMM: Predict → Run → Investigate → Modify → Make) (20 min).** Fill in the Problem, Plan and Data headings; run the cleaning cells.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What must you cite for the limit?
2. Why plan for missing data?
3. What is a one-line question?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 2: Analyse and publish

**Hook (5 min).** Two minutes to tell your city something true about its air.

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Summarise by month; chart; write claim–evidence–reasoning–limit; optionally build the dashboard.

**Activity (PRIMM: Predict → Run → Investigate → Modify → Make) (20 min).** Complete the analysis; peer review using stars and wishes; oral check.

**Check (5 min).**
1. Three parts of a conclusion?
2. Name two limits in this study.
3. Why disclose AI use?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?
