# Unit project — Air in my city

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
Choose a question about air quality in your city.

## Plan
PPDAC plan card.

## Data
Air table + cleaning log.

## Analysis
Monthly summary + one chart (+ optional dashboard).

## Conclusion
Claim, evidence, limits.

## Hand in
Notebook/dashboard + poster + AI-use line (Grade 10 flagship).

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
