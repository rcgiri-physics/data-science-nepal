# Grade 10 · Unit 6: AI literacy and ethics

> Track: A (no-code) · Tools: discussion, case cards · Lessons: 3 · Version: 0.1 draft (English)
> Datasets: none

**Big idea.** AI systems are built from data and choices. Understanding where they fail helps us use them wisely and fairly.

**What you will be able to do**

* I can describe how an AI system learns from data.
* I can explain hallucination and bias with examples.
* I can follow a responsible-use checklist.

**Words to know:** AI, training data, bias, hallucination, transparency, accountability (Nepali glossary: `curriculum/framework/glossary.md`).

---

## Lesson 1: How AI learns

**Hook (5 min).** Your phone suggests the next word. How does it guess?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Many AI systems find patterns in huge amounts of examples (training data) and then predict. They don't understand like humans.

**Activity (20 min).** Play 'predict the next word' with a class story; discuss how more examples change guesses.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What does an AI learn from?
2. Does it understand like a person?
3. What happens with bad data?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 2: Hallucination and bias

**Hook (5 min).** An AI gives a confident answer with a made-up source. Why?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Systems generate plausible output, not guaranteed truth. They may also reflect biases in data (language, gender, region).

**Activity (20 min).** Verify five AI-claimed 'facts' using the dataset card or an official site; log which were wrong.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What is hallucination?
2. How do you check?
3. Give a source of bias.

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 3: Responsible use

**Hook (5 min).** Should a student use AI for homework? When is it helpful, when harmful?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Use AI as a tutor, disclose its use, protect personal data, and be able to explain your work. Rules differ for high-stakes decisions.

**Activity (20 min).** Write your own class AI charter (6 rules) using the AI-use policy as a starting point.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What does 'explain, don't answer' mean?
2. What must you never paste into a tool?
3. Why an oral check?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?
