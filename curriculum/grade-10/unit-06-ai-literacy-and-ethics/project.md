# Unit project — AI charter and audit

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
How should our class use AI responsibly?

## Plan
Read the policy.

## Data
Your own AI-use log from a week.

## Analysis
Where did it help? where was it wrong?

## Conclusion
Class charter with six rules.

## Hand in
Charter + audit log.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
