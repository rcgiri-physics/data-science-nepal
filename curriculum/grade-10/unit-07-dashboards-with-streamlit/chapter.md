# Grade 10 · Unit 7: Dashboards with Streamlit

> Track: B (code) · Tools: Python, Streamlit · Lessons: 3 · Version: 0.1 draft (English)
> Datasets: `kathmandu-air-quality`

**Big idea.** A dashboard lets others explore your analysis. Good dashboards answer one question clearly and say where the data came from.

**What you will be able to do**

* I can run a Streamlit app locally.
* I can add a selector and chart to the app.
* I can write a clear title, note on source and limitations.

**Words to know:** dashboard, widget, app, source note (Nepali glossary: `curriculum/framework/glossary.md`).

---

## Lesson 1: Design on paper

**Hook (5 min).** You have 10 seconds. What must a visitor understand?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Pick one question; sketch title, key number, chart, selector and a source/limitations note.

**Activity (20 min).** Paper-prototype a dashboard; test with a classmate.

**Check (5 min).**
1. What is the first design decision?
2. Why add a source note?
3. How many main messages?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 2: Build with Streamlit

**Hook (5 min).** How do you turn a notebook into a page anyone can use?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Streamlit turns a Python script into a web app: st.title, st.sidebar selectors, st.line_chart, st.metric. The script reruns when widgets change.

**Activity (PRIMM: Predict → Run → Investigate → Modify → Make) (20 min).** Run the starter app; change the title; add a month filter.

**Check (5 min).**
1. How do you run an app?
2. What does st.sidebar.selectbox do?
3. What does the script do when you click?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?

## Lesson 3: Publish and review

**Hook (5 min).** Is it ready for strangers?

**Concept (≤ 5 min).** Check: clear title, units, source, date, limits, no personal data, works on a phone. Free hosting options exist, but data licences still apply.

**Activity (20 min).** Use the dashboard checklist to review a partner's app; fix three issues.

**Check (5 min).**
1. Name two things to check before sharing.
2. Does hosting change the data's licence rules?
3. Why test on a phone?

**Reflect (2 min).** What surprised me? Who or what is missing from this data?
