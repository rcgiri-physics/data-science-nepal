# Grade 10 — Data analysts (Python and analysis)

**Tools:** Python (pandas, matplotlib), SQL, Streamlit · **Units:** 8 · **Lessons:** 24 (+ projects)

Grade 10 moves from wrangling to *analysis* in Python: the pandas toolkit on a messy air-quality series, distributions and
outliers, relationships and the line of best fit, time series, a first honest look at what a *model* is (k-nearest neighbours, unplugged
then coded), AI literacy and ethics, and a small Streamlit dashboard. Notebooks run offline on **synthetic** look-alike tables;
swap in real data with `tools/fetch_data.py` after checking the licence.
