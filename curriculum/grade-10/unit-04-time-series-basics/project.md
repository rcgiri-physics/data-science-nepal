# Unit project — Climate or weather?

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
Is there a warming trend in this table?

## Plan
State what would count as a trend.

## Data
Monthly climate table (synthetic/real CCKP).

## Analysis
Annual means, slope per decade, anomalies.

## Conclusion
Claim with uncertainty and doubts.

## Hand in
Notebook + paragraph.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
