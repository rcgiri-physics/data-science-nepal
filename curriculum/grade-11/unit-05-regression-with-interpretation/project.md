# Unit project — Explain it, don't just fit it

**Time:** 2 periods + homework. **Groups of 3** with roles: data steward, analyst/driver, storyteller (rotate).

## Problem
Choose an outcome and 2 predictors.

## Plan
Predict signs of coefficients.

## Data
Health table (synthetic).

## Analysis
Fit, interpret, residuals.

## Conclusion
Plain-language interpretation with limits.

## Hand in
Notebook + paragraph.

**Rubric:** the six-criterion four-level rubric in `docs/pedagogy/assessment.md`. **AI help:** disclose in one line.
