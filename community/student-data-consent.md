# Student data: consent and anonymisation checklist

Use before any class survey, school weather station or student-generated dataset is added to a lesson or to this repository.

## Before collecting
- [ ] Teacher/school approved the activity.
- [ ] Students (and guardians where required) were told **why** we collect, **what** we will do with it, and that saying no is fine.
- [ ] We collect only what the question needs (no names, no exact addresses, no sensitive fields without a strong reason).
- [ ] Every student gets a **code** (S01, S02…); the code–name list is kept by the teacher only and destroyed after the project.

## Before sharing or publishing
- [ ] No names, photos, phone numbers or exact locations.
- [ ] Small groups (fewer than 5 students) merged or hidden so nobody can be recognised.
- [ ] Guardian/school permission to publish.
- [ ] Dataset card written (`docs/templates/DATASET_CARD_TEMPLATE.md`); licence CC0 or CC BY.

## Consent line (copy, translate, print)
> "We are collecting information about our class for a school project on ______. You will not be named. You may say no to any question.
> The results will be shared as ______." — Teacher: ______ Date: ______
