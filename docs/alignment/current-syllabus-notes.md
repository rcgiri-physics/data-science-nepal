# Current Nepali syllabus context

Summary of what public sources report about data and computing in the current curriculum.

| Stage | Subject | What is reported | What this curriculum adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gr 8 | Compulsory Maths – Statistics | Pie chart, mean, median, mode; textbook is calculation-heavy ([Springer analysis](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-97-8426-4_24)) | Ask → collect → analyse → interpret; misleading graphs; data ethics |
| Gr 9–10 | **Optional** Computer Science (2080) | Gr 9 mentions SQL; Gr 10 Python, DBMS, AI & contemporary technology | Applied data analysis on real data; PPDAC; dashboards |
| Gr 11–12 | NEB Computer Science | DBMS, web, C, OOP, trends; no Python or data analysis | Enrichment units mapped to NEB topics |
| 2026 | CDC curriculum revision | Task force from Apr 2026; consultations to 16 Sep 2026; "AI and ICT" thematic committee ([Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/09/14/nepal-begins-overhaul-of-school-curriculum-with-ai-and-indigenous-knowledge-in-focus)) | Adoptable unit pack |

**Design consequence:** because Computer Science is optional, Track A (no-code, inside compulsory Maths, Science and Social
studies) carries Grades 8–10; Track B (code) is for CS students, clubs and Grades 11–12. Every Track B unit has an unplugged
alternative or prerequisite.

*This summary is based on published descriptions of the syllabi rather than page-level citations of the syllabus documents.*
