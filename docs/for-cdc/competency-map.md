## Competency statements (draft, "I can…")
* **Ask:** I can form a question that data can answer.
* **Collect:** I can collect or choose data fairly and with consent.
* **Analyse:** I can summarise, compare and model data with suitable tools.
* **Interpret:** I can draw a claim supported by evidence and state its limits.
* **Communicate:** I can present findings clearly to a real audience.
* **Act responsibly:** I can protect people's privacy, check fairness and disclose AI use.
