# Blog post draft — "Data science for every Nepali student: an open curriculum"

*(Draft for the maintainer's blog; replace REPO_URL when the repository is public.)*

Nepal is rewriting its school curriculum. Students will grow up with data and AI around them — in health, weather, markets and
social media — yet most never learn to ask a good data question, collect data fairly, or tell when a graph is misleading.

I am building **Data Science Nepal (डेटा विज्ञान)**: a free, open, Grades 8–12 curriculum on Nepali data, for teachers and students.

**What's inside**
* 5 grades × 8 units, each with a student chapter, teacher guide, project, quiz and (from Grade 9) runnable notebooks.
* A **no-code track** (paper, spreadsheet, CODAP) for all students and a **code track** (SQL, Python) for clubs and optional Computer Science.
* Offline delivery for schools with weak internet.
* Real-data pipelines — fetched from the original publishers with licences recorded — and clearly labelled synthetic practice tables.
* Ethics and responsible AI use from the first unit.

**How you can help:** teachers (try a unit and tell us what happened), statisticians (review), translators, and anyone with open Nepali data.

**Reuse:** content is CC BY 4.0, code is MIT. Please credit "Data Science Nepal by Giri Ramchandra and contributors" with a link to REPO_URL.

Status: v0.1, not yet classroom-tested. If you want to pilot in your school, open an issue.
