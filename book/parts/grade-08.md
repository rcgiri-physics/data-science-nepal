# Part 1 — Grade 8: Data detectives

**No code. Paper, calculator, and (optionally) a spreadsheet or CODAP.**

Grade 8 builds the habit at the heart of all data work: a **statistical investigation**. You will ask questions that
data can answer, collect data fairly and with consent, record it in tidy tables, draw graphs that tell the truth,
summarise and compare groups, tell a story with evidence — and ask who is missing. Your Grade 8 Maths book teaches you to
calculate a mean and draw a pie chart; this part teaches you what those numbers and pictures are *for*.

table, and becomes the flagship poster in Unit 7.

**Pacing:** 24 lessons + 8 projects ≈ 40 periods (one a week for a year). Short on time? Units 1–5 and 7 are the core.

**By the end of Grade 8** you should be able to tick every box in the "ready for Grade 9?" checklist at the end of
Unit 8.
