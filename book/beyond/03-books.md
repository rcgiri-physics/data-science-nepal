# 3. Books to read

Books are grouped by purpose and level. **Free** means the authors or publishers offer a legal free online version (check
the official site; licences vary, often non-commercial). Paid books are often available in libraries or as second-hand
copies. Start with one book per area; finishing one good book beats starting five.

Level: **●○○** right after Grade 12 · **●●○** first/second year of university · **●●●** advanced

|---|---|---|---|---|

| *Weapons of Math Destruction* | Cathy O'Neil | no | ●○○ | how algorithms can harm people |
| *Data Feminism* | Catherine D'Ignazio, Lauren Klein | yes (open access) | ●○○ | power, fairness and who counts in data |

## A reading order

1. *The Art of Statistics* (or *Factfulness*) — for perspective.
2. *Think Python* or *Python for Data Analysis* — alongside practice.
3. *OpenIntro Statistics* — the statistics foundation.
4. *An Introduction to Statistical Learning* — machine learning properly.
5. *Mathematics for Machine Learning* — when you want the "why" behind the methods.
6. Then follow your chosen direction.
