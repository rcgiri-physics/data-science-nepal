# Health practice table (SYNTHETIC)

**SYNTHETIC: 1,500 invented mother-child records.** Real DHS microdata may NOT be redistributed (DHS terms:
no re-distribution "directly or within any tool/dashboard"; use limited to the registered purpose). That is why
this table exists. A teacher or student who wants real DHS data must register at dhsprogram.com themselves.

## Columns
`record_id, ecological_belt, mother_age, wealth_quintile (1 poorest - 5 richest), antenatal_visits, institutional_delivery (0/1), birth_weight_kg`.

## Ethics
Relationships here were *built in* by the generator (wealth and visits raise delivery in a facility). Students should
state that findings from a synthetic table say nothing about real women, and discuss why that matters.
